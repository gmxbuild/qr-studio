About QR Studio

QR Studio is a free, professional-grade QR code generator that runs entirely in your web browser. No signup, no email, no tracking — just fast, private, and reliable QR codes.

Our Mission

We believe that essential tools should be free and accessible to everyone. Whether you're a small business owner creating a menu QR, a teacher sharing a Wi-Fi password, or a marketer promoting a social profile — you deserve a tool that works instantly without limits or hidden fees.

What Makes Us Different

Unlike most online QR generators, QR Studio processes everything on your own device. Your data never leaves your phone or computer. There is no server-side storage, no analytics tracking of your QR content, and no account required.

Features

We support 15+ QR code types including website URLs, plain text, Wi-Fi networks, WhatsApp messages, email, phone numbers, SMS, Instagram, TikTok, Facebook, YouTube, X (Twitter), Telegram, contact cards (vCard), and geographic locations. Every code can be customized with your own colors and size, and downloaded as a high-resolution PNG.

Lifetime Validity

All QR codes generated through QR Studio are static and never expire. They will continue to work as long as the destination (website, phone number, network) remains available. No subscriptions, no renewals, no expiry dates.
