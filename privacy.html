Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2025

Your privacy is critically important to us. This policy explains what information QR Studio collects (spoiler: almost none) and how we handle it.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

QR Studio does not require an account, email address, or any personal information to use. We do not collect, store, transmit, or share:

  • Content you enter into the QR generator (URLs, text, Wi-Fi passwords, contact details, etc.)
  • QR codes you generate
  • Your name, email, phone number, or address
  • Your location

All QR code generation happens locally in your browser using JavaScript. Your data never leaves your device.

2. Local Storage

QR Studio uses your browser's local storage to remember your last 50 generated QR codes (so you can revisit them from the History panel). This data stays on your device and is never transmitted to any server. You can clear it anytime from the History panel or by clearing your browser data.

3. Analytics and Advertising

QR Studio may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

We do not run any first-party analytics or tracking scripts.

4. Third-Party Libraries

QR Studio loads the open-source QR code generation library from a CDN (jsDelivr or unpkg). These CDNs may log standard web requests (IP address, user agent) as part of normal operation. We do not control or access that data.

5. Children's Privacy

QR Studio is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

7. Contact

Questions about this policy? Contact us at our Contact page.
